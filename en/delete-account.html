Account and Data Deletion Request

You can delete your WallWall account directly in the app. If you cannot use the app or need help, you can also request deletion by email.

Deleting in the app

  • Open the WallWall app.
  • Go to the Settings tab and select account management.
  • Select account deletion and confirm as prompted.

Requesting by email

If you cannot sign in to the app, please email supports@amuse8.kr to request account deletion. So that we can verify your identity, include the login provider you used (Google, Apple, or Kakao) and the email address on the account.

What is deleted

  • Your WallWall account information
  • Your nickname, profile photo, and the account details provided by your social login
  • Settings stored on the account, such as followed sectors and language preference
  • Login session information

Records that must be kept under applicable law, and limited records kept in a form that does not directly identify you for security or error analysis, may be retained for a limited period in accordance with the relevant laws and our Privacy Policy.
