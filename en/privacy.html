Privacy Policy

The U.S. and Korean economic news short-form video and card-style content platform (the "Service") developed by Amuse8 (the "Company," "we," or "us") establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy pursuant to Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act, in order to protect the personal information of data subjects and to handle related grievances promptly and effectively.

Article 1 — Purposes of Processing Personal Information

The Company processes personal information for the following purposes. The personal information processed will not be used for any purpose other than those listed below, and if the purpose changes, separate consent will be obtained in accordance with Article 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act.

  • Providing and operating the Service
  • Providing social login, account creation, and account management
  • Syncing user settings such as followed sectors and language
  • Providing the AI assistant (questions about news articles and your conversation history), managing usage limits, and preventing abuse
  • Reviewing feedback on AI answers that users submit voluntarily
  • Improving and optimizing Service quality
  • Collecting and analyzing app errors (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Analyzing Service usage statistics
  • Measuring advertising performance and optimizing ad delivery
  • Handling customer inquiries and complaints

Article 2 — Categories of Personal Information Processed

Information Collected When You Create or Use an Account

Login is optional, and public content remains available without logging in. If you choose to sign in with Google, Apple, or Kakao, we collect the login provider, provider account identifier, email address if provided, nickname or display name, and profile image URL if provided. If you edit your nickname or upload a profile photo, we also process that nickname and profile photo.

Service Settings

  • Followed news sectors and market sectors
  • Service language setting
  • Authentication token information used to keep your session secure

Information Processed When You Use the AI Assistant

The AI assistant is an optional feature available to logged-in users from the news detail screen. When you use it, we process the following:

  • The questions you enter, the generated answers, and the sources cited in those answers
  • Earlier questions and answers within the same conversation (conversation context)
  • Account identifier, conversation and request identifiers, and usage-limit records such as the number of questions asked
  • Feedback you submit voluntarily: your rating, any comment you enter, the rated answer with its question and the immediately preceding exchange, and the article card the answer was based on

To answer a question, the question, the conversation context, and relevant news context are sent to OpenAI as described in Article 7. Account information such as your email address, nickname, followed sectors, or holdings is not sent.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information: device model, OS version, app version, unique device identifier
  • Usage records: access time, IP address, approximate location (country/region), screen views, taps, shares, search terms, and other Service usage history
  • Error information: app crash information, error logs

Information Collected for Advertising Measurement

  • App events such as app installation and app launch
  • App information, device information, and the IP address used for the network connection
  • App Tracking Transparency permission status
  • Device advertising identifier, only where available under the operating system's privacy settings

Article 6 describes in detail how this information is used and transferred.

Article 3 — Processing and Retention Period of Personal Information

  • Account information and synced settings: retained until account deletion, unless retention is required by applicable law
  • Profile photos: retained until you remove the photo or delete your account
  • Authentication token information: retained until logout, account deletion, or token expiration
  • AI conversation content: retained until you delete the conversation or delete your account, and removed automatically 90 days after the last message
  • AI answer feedback: retained for 90 days after submission
  • AI usage-limit records (no conversation content): retained until 48 hours after the applicable daily or monthly limit period ends
  • Service usage analytics: retained for up to 14 months after collection
  • Error analysis data: retained according to Firebase Crashlytics retention policies
  • Advertising measurement data: retained for the period necessary for the purposes in Article 6, in accordance with the retention practices of Meta Platforms, Inc.
  • Inquiry records: retained for 3 years after the inquiry is resolved (statutory retention obligation)

Article 4 — Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

As a rule, the Company does not provide users' personal information to external parties. Information is provided only in the following cases:

  • When the user has given prior consent
  • When the categories set out in Article 6 are provided to Meta Platforms, Inc. for advertising measurement, within the scope described in that article
  • When required by law, or when an investigative agency requests it for investigative purposes in accordance with the procedures and methods prescribed by law

Article 5 — Outsourcing of Personal Information Processing

The Company outsources personal information processing tasks as follows in order to provide the Service:

  • Google LLC (Firebase): app error collection, usage analytics, and remote configuration
  • Google LLC, Apple Inc., Kakao Corp.: social login authentication selected by the user
  • Amazon Web Services, Inc.: profile image storage and service data storage
  • OpenAI OpCo, LLC: generating AI assistant answers and preventing abuse

Article 6 — Meta Advertising Measurement

The Company uses the Meta SDK provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. in order to measure app installations and use attributable to the Company's advertising and to improve advertising performance. Through this SDK, app events such as app installation, app launch, opening news or video content, and completing a news search, together with app information, device information, and the IP address used for the network connection, may be sent to Meta.

On iOS, WallWall requests permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency system before enabling advertiser tracking and the collection of the device advertising identifier. If you decline, every feature of the Service remains available to you, and in that case only limited app events and aggregated installation attribution that do not use the advertising identifier may be processed.

The Company does not send your account identifier, email address, search terms, followed sectors or watchlist contents, or the titles of the articles you read to Meta.

  • Recipient: Meta Platforms, Inc.
  • Purpose: advertising attribution, campaign measurement, fraud prevention, and ad delivery optimization
  • Information transferred: app events such as installation and launch; app information; device information; network information including the IP address; the advertiser-tracking choice applied from the iOS App Tracking Transparency decision; and the device advertising identifier where available under the operating system's privacy settings
  • Timing and method: transmitted over an encrypted connection when the app is launched or a configured event occurs
  • Processing location: the United States and other countries in which Meta processes data
  • Retention and use period: for the period necessary for the purposes above, subject to Meta's Business Tools terms and privacy policy

On iOS you can review or change tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android you can delete your advertising ID or limit personalized advertising in your device's ad settings. Declining or later withdrawing tracking permission does not restrict your use of the Service.

Article 7 — Overseas Transfer of Personal Information

The Company transfers personal information overseas as follows in order to provide the Service:

  • Recipients: Google LLC, Apple Inc., Amazon Web Services, Inc., Meta Platforms, Inc.
  • Countries of transfer: the United States and other countries where each processor or recipient operates its services
  • Purpose of transfer: app error analysis, usage analytics, social login authentication, profile image storage, Service operation, and advertising measurement
  • Categories of data transferred: account identifiers, email address if provided, profile information, device information, approximate location, usage records, error logs, app events, and the device advertising identifier where you have allowed tracking
  • Retention and use period: as described in this Policy and in each processor's or recipient's retention policies

Article 6 governs the details of what is transferred to Meta Platforms, Inc., why, and the choices available to you.

When you use the AI assistant, personal information is transferred overseas as follows. Nothing below is sent unless you use the AI assistant, and not using it does not restrict any other part of the Service.

  • Recipient: OpenAI OpCo, LLC (1455 3rd Street, San Francisco, CA 94158, United States, privacy@openai.com)
  • Countries of transfer: the United States and the countries where OpenAI's sub-processors are located (OpenAI sub-processor list)
  • Purpose of transfer: generating AI assistant answers and preventing abuse
  • Categories of data transferred: your question, earlier questions and answers in the same conversation, and relevant news context
  • Timing and method: transmitted over an encrypted connection each time you send a question
  • Retention and use period: OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models by default and may retain it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring
